Privacy policy

Frostbites · last updated 25 September 2026

The short version

Frostbites does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign in, and the game does not send anything about you anywhere.

What is kept, and where

Your save (your shop, your progress and your settings) is written to storage on your own device and stays there. It is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it.

What is not collected

  • No name, email address, phone number or account of any kind.
  • No location, contacts, photos, files or device identifiers.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. There are no third party advertising networks in the app.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not use the camera, the microphone, location or storage outside its own private area.

Purchases

This release contains no in-app purchases. If purchases are added in a future version, payment is handled entirely by Google Play and this policy will be updated to say what that involves. Frostbites would still not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top.
